Media and expertise

I am happy to help journalists and science writers understand viral infections and the diagnostic tools used to identify them, in French or in English. Please write to jacques.fourgeaud@aphp.fr with your outlet, topic and deadline; I usually reply within two days. Until February 2027 I am based in San Francisco (Pacific Time) and can do phone or video interviews; afterwards I am back in Paris.

Topics I can comment on

  • Emerging and unusual viral infections: new viruses identified in humans, viruses jumping from animals to humans, One Health investigations.
  • Clinical metagenomics and genomic diagnostics: how unbiased sequencing identifies the cause of unexplained infections, and what it can and cannot tell.
  • Infections in immunocompromised patients: transplant recipients and children with inborn errors of immunity.
  • Cytomegalovirus (CMV) in pregnancy and newborns: screening, prevention, prenatal diagnosis and treatment, including first-trimester screening in France.
  • Viral infections in pregnancy and childhood: congenital infections and pediatric virology.

Biography (150 words)

Jacques Fourgeaud, PharmD, PhD, is a clinical virologist and Associate Professor of Clinical Virology (MCU-PH) at Université Paris Cité and Necker-Enfants Malades Hospital (AP-HP), France's national pediatric and rare-disease referral center. He heads the hospital's clinical metagenomics program, one of the few laboratories in France offering this unbiased sequencing approach as a routine diagnostic service, accredited to ISO 15189 since 2025. His work has led to the identification or first clinical description of several viral diseases, including the first human circovirus causing hepatitis and occult rat hepatitis E virus infection as a cause of cirrhosis. He is deputy head of the laboratory associated with the French National Reference Center for congenital cytomegalovirus infection. Trained as a pharmacist and medical biologist, he holds an MSc from Institut Pasteur and a PhD in virology. In 2026-2027 he is a Visiting Scholar at the University of California, San Francisco. He has authored 59 peer-reviewed publications.
