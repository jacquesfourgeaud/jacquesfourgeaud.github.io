For clinicians: sending a sample for clinical metagenomics

Our laboratory offers pathogen detection by metagenomic sequencing (NGS) on all sample types, including for patients managed in other hospitals. Before sending any sample, the indication must be discussed with a microbiologist of our team: samples from other institutions are accepted only after prior agreement. How to reach the team, sample requirements, shipping conditions and the request form are described in the Necker laboratory sampling manual (in French).
