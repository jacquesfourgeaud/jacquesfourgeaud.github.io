I am an Associate Professor of Clinical Virology (Maître de Conférences des Universités - Praticien Hospitalier, MCU-PH) at Université Paris Cité and Necker-Enfants Malades Hospital (AP-HP) in Paris, France's national pediatric and rare-disease referral center. I head the hospital's clinical metagenomics program, one of only four laboratories in France holding the national reference label (LBMR) for clinical metagenomics, and I am deputy head of the laboratory associated with the French National Reference Center for congenital cytomegalovirus infection.

I trained as a pharmacist (PharmD, Université de Limoges) and completed a residency in medical biology with a specialty in microbiology, including rotations at Bordeaux University Hospital, the arbovirus reference center of Institut Pasteur de Cayenne (French Guiana), and Mayotte Hospital in the Indian Ocean. I then obtained an MSc in virology at Institut Pasteur, where I worked in the Pathogen Discovery Laboratory, and a PhD in virology from Université Paris Cité on the identification of viruses involved in unexplained diseases of immunocompromised patients. In 2024 I spent six months in Prof. Judith Breuer's laboratory at the UCL Great Ormond Street Institute of Child Health in London, working on cytomegalovirus whole-genome sequencing.

I am currently spending a research year (February 2026 to February 2027) as a Visiting Scholar in the laboratory of Prof. Charles Chiu at the University of California, San Francisco, developing host and pathogen metatranscriptomics for unexplained fetal and perinatal disease.

My research sits at the interface of clinical virology, genomics and translational medicine. I use unbiased sequencing (clinical metagenomics and metatranscriptomics) as a discovery and diagnostic engine, characterize the emerging and unusual viral diseases it reveals, focus on vulnerable hosts (immunocompromised patients, the fetus and the neonate), and translate the results into accredited diagnostics, treatment decisions and screening policy. My core research themes are detailed in the Research tab, and a selection of papers in the Publications tab.

Highlights of this work include:

  • A three-year national prospective evaluation of clinical metagenomics in France (742 samples, 523 patients), published in The Lancet Microbe (2024).
  • The identification or first clinical characterization of several viral diseases: the first hepatotropic human circovirus (HCirV-1, Emerg Infect Dis 2023), chronic Aichi virus disease in inborn errors of immunity (Clin Infect Dis 2023), occult rat hepatitis E virus infection as a cause of cirrhosis and post-transplant recurrence (Am J Transplant 2026), and fatal pigeon paramyxovirus type 1 encephalitis (Emerg Infect Dis 2026).
  • Reference-level contributions to congenital CMV diagnosis and prevention, including neonatal prognostic factors in the multicenter Cymepedia cohort (Pediatrics 2024) and vertical transmission risk according to IgG avidity and valacyclovir treatment (Clin Infect Dis 2025), at the time France adopted universal first-trimester CMV screening.
  • Leading the clinical metagenomics program of Necker-Enfants Malades Hospital to national reference status (LBMR label, 2024) and ISO 15189 accreditation (2025), with more than 2,500 clinically indicated samples analyzed since 2019.
